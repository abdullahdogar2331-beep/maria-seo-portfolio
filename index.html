A little about me
Hello, there.

I learn what your customers search for and help your website show up for it.

SEO should feel human — because it is.

I’m Maria Ali, an SEO specialist. I start by learning what your customers need and what they type into Google. Then I help make your website more useful and easier to find.

I can help choose useful keywords, improve page titles and content, and find website issues that may make it harder to appear in search results.

What I can help with

Small details. Stronger foundations.

SEO means making helpful improvements so people can find and use your website more easily.

01 / DISCOVER↗

Keyword research

Find the words people search for and choose the best pages to target them.

02 / IMPROVE↗

On-page SEO

Improve page titles, descriptions, headings, and links so pages are clear and easy to understand.

03 / STRENGTHEN↗

Technical SEO basics

Check whether Google can find your pages and whether your website works well on phones.

04 / PLAN↗

Content strategy

Plan useful articles and pages based on the questions your customers ask.

05 / OPTIMIZE↗

Website SEO audit

Review your website, find problems, and list the most important fixes first.

06 / LEARN↗

SEO reporting

Check website visits and search performance, then use the numbers to plan the next steps.

How I work

A clear path from question to action.

I keep the process simple: understand your goals, explain what needs work, and agree on clear next steps.

STEP 01

Listen

STEP 02

Research

STEP 03

STEP 04
